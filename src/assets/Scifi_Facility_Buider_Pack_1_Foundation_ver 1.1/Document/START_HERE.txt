RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW

The fastest way to learn the pack:

1. Open the Preview folder.
2. Choose one of the example rooms.
3. Use it as a reference.
4. Rebuild it with the included assets.
5. Expand it into your own layout.


BUILD ORDER

Build from the environment structure outward:

1. FLOOR
Lay out the room footprint.

2. FLOOR EDGE TRIM (OPTIONAL)
Add along the top wall where needed for a finished edge or additional detail.

3. WALLS
Build the room boundary using Top / Front Walls, Corner Walls, Side Walls, and Divider Walls.

4. DOOR
Place the appropriate door and threshold.

5. FIXED PROPS
Add wall and floor-mounted elements such as Pipes, Electrical Boxes, Alarm Lights, Security Cameras, Wall Vents, Access Panels, Drains, and Hatches.

6. FREESTANDING PROPS
Add Benches, Shelves, and other movable props.

7. MARKINGS & SIGNAGE
Add Floor Arrows, Room Labels, Direction Signs, Warning Signs, and Hazard Markings.

8. MOOD
Finish with Rust, Grime, Dirt, Blood, and Light Glow overlays.


WALL SYSTEM

The Top and Front Walls use single modules and can be used independently for open areas, corridors, or facility boundaries.

For enclosed rooms, use four Corner modules to define the room boundaries:

[Corner] ───────────── [Corner]
    │                       │
    │                       │
    │                       │
[Corner] ───────────── [Corner]

Connect Side Wall Repeat modules between the Corner modules:

[Corner] [Repeat] [Repeat] [Corner]

Wall layering:

Place the Top Wall first. Then place the Corner modules and Side Wall Front Wall Repeats.

Corner modules handle the wall junctions and preserve the intended shadow and edge alignment.


DIVIDER WALL

Divider Walls are available in Horizontal and Vertical orientations. Each orientation uses its own Start and End modules, while the central Repeat module is shared.


Two wall zones are included:

W1 STERILE PANEL
Best for Lab, Medical, and Office spaces.

W2 PAINTED METAL
Best for Utility, Maintenance, and Industrial spaces.

Use one wall zone as the primary visual language of a room. Mix zones only when intentional.


FLOOR SYSTEM

Three floor types are included:

F1 STERILE TILE
Best for Lab, Medical, and Office spaces.

F2 PAINTED STEEL
Best for Utility, Maintenance, and Corridor spaces.

F3 GRATED METAL
Best for Engineering and heavy machinery areas.


Floors can be mixed within one room. For clarity, use a maximum of two floor types per room.

Tie the secondary floor to a specific function, such as an equipment area, walkway, or loading zone. Avoid scattering floor types randomly.


FLOOR EDGE TRIM

Place Floor Edge Trim along the top edge of the floor, directly beneath the Top Wall.

It is a separate module rather than part of the wall or floor.

Floor Edge Trim represents a service trench where cables, pipes, and utilities connect the walls to the floor.


DOOR SYSTEM

Two doors are included:

D1 STANDARD SLIDING DOOR
General-purpose door for hallways, offices, and other facility spaces.

D2 LABORATORY DOOR
Designed for research and medical spaces, with an observation window.


Both includes:

- Closed state
- Open state

Doors are placed on the Top Wall in this pack.
The hazard stripe sits at the door base. The threshold strip marks the passage on the floor side.
